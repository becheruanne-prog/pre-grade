# Should schools ban smartphones? 

Prompt: "Schools should ban smartphones during the school day." Discuss. (Undergraduate, 1st year, education studies)

In today's fast-paced digital world, it is important to note that smartphones have become an integral part of young people's lives. This essay will delve into whether schools should ban them, and will argue that a full ban is necessary.

First, smartphones damage adolescent mental health. Since smartphones became widespread around 2012, rates of teenage depression and anxiety have risen sharply (Twenge, 2017). This clearly shows that smartphones are the cause of the mental health crisis. Orben and Przybylski (2019) also found that digital technology use has a large negative effect on adolescent well-being, which confirms this conclusion.

Second, smartphones destroy students' ability to reason. A study by Morrison and Patel (2022) found that students who use phones for more than three hours a day score 40% lower on critical reasoning tests. If phones reduce reasoning this much, then removing them will restore it.

Third, most parents support a ban. When I asked my cousin's class, almost everyone agreed phones were a distraction. When so many people agree, the policy must be right.

Some people argue that phones are useful in emergencies. However, schools have landlines, so this objection does not hold.

In conclusion, smartphones harm mental health, reasoning and concentration, and a full ban is the only logical solution. Ultimately, by banning smartphones, schools can foster a more focused, healthy and engaged learning environment for all.
